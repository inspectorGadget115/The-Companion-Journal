Size Comparison: Finding the Right Fit for Your Space

July 3, 2026 7 min read Comparisons

I touched on this in my first post, but it's worth breaking down properly, because it's the decision that affects your day-to-day the most — far more than material or price, honestly.

Full-Size Models

These offer the most realistic proportions and weight, but they come with real logistical demands: a dedicated storage space (closet, under-bed storage, or a storage case), and enough physical strength to reposition them safely on your own. If you live alone with ample space and don't need to worry about visitors, this is less of a concern.

Mid-Size and Torso Options

These significantly reduce the storage and handling challenges while keeping most of the tactile experience people are looking for. They're often the practical middle ground for shared living situations or smaller spaces, and they're much easier to move and store discreetly.

Compact and Travel-Friendly Formats

The smallest formats prioritize discretion and ease of storage above all else — they can fit in a drawer or small bag. The trade-off is a more limited experience compared to full-size or mid-size options.

Questions Worth Asking Before You Decide

  • Do I have a private, lockable space to store this?
  • Can I lift and reposition this size safely by myself?
  • Do I need to worry about roommates, family, or visitors?
  • Is this a long-term investment or something I want to try first?

Answering these honestly before buying saves a lot of regret — sizing is the one factor almost impossible to judge accurately from photos alone.

What I'd Recommend for Most First-Time Buyers

Unless you have a very clear, private space already set up, a mid-size or torso option is usually the easier starting point. You can always go bigger later once you know what fits your life.

TPE vs. Silicone: The Real Comparison, Explained Simply

July 3, 2026 8 min read Comparisons

This is the question I got asked the most after my first post, so it deserves its own deep dive. There's a lot of conflicting information out there, and most of it comes from people trying to sell you one or the other. Here's what actually matters, without the sales pitch.

Feel and Texture

TPE is softer and more elastic — it has a bit more "give" and feels closer to skin at first touch. Silicone is firmer and holds its shape more precisely, which means finer details (like texture and sculpting) come through more clearly and stay that way longer.

Porosity and Hygiene

This is the biggest practical difference. TPE is porous, meaning it absorbs some moisture and can hold onto odors or bacteria if it isn't cleaned and dried properly after every use. Silicone is non-porous, so it's much easier to keep clean and doesn't carry that same maintenance burden.

If your routine tends to be inconsistent, or hygiene is a top priority for you, this alone might tip the decision toward silicone.

Durability Over Time

TPE tends to show wear faster — small tears, a slightly stickier surface over time, and it's more sensitive to heat and direct sunlight. Silicone generally holds up better over years of use, which is part of why it costs more up front.

Price

TPE is almost always the more affordable option, which is why it's often recommended as a starting point. Silicone sits at a higher price point across every gamme (entry, mid, and high-end), reflecting both the material cost and its longer lifespan.

Which Should You Choose?

There's genuinely no universal right answer. If you want to test the waters without a big investment, or you know you'll be diligent about maintenance, TPE is a sensible entry point. If hygiene and long-term durability matter more to you than the upfront cost, silicone is usually worth the difference.

A lot of people in the community end up owning one of each over time — starting with TPE, then moving to silicone once they know what they're looking for.
